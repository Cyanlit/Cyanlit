Reflection

Why this design
I went with a minimal, terminal-inspired layout because it matches how I think about CS: dense with information, but ordered and deliberate. Beyond the aesthetic, I wanted every section to be scannable at a glance, since I know reviewers and recruiters often skim. At the same time, I cared about getting the small things right — consistent spacing, hover states, and alignment — because I think attention to detail in a layout reflects the same care I try to bring to code.
Next steps
The site currently shows what I've built, but not how I think. The next addition will be a blog or notes section where I write up technical posts — things like design decisions behind my projects, concepts I'm learning, or problems I've debugged. I think that will give a better sense of my process, not just the end results.
